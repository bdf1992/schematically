## What changed

Each gap became a measure with a planted failing case (tests/layout_quality_qa.py), then the
defect was fixed where it lived.

| Gap named | Measure (src/57-layout-metrics.js) | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 02: "RETURN?" reads as a code | `code-label`: capitals ending in ? or ! | the reciprocity mark says `return expected` / `return required` in words |
| 03, 04, 07: containers mostly empty | `empty-container`: children under 20% of the interior | the three containers (and 08's) sized to their contents |
| 04, 06: the route goes the long way | `route-wraps`: a route running outside every card it connects | 06: WITNESS placed left of EVENT |
| 11, 12: labels pressed against lines and cards | `cramped-label`: clearance under 0.6 of the label's font size | labels inside a section sit 11px above the core; 12's sources moved clear of the skin labels |
| 08: the control tick is faint; 09: marks crowd the fork; 10: a join drawn as a splice | not yet measured | carried to the next review |

Where the measure disagreed with the reviewer, the measure was kept and the claim corrected:
"over twice the Manhattan distance" (04, 06) is false for both, whose routes run 1.2x their
ends' distance. What reads as a detour is a route leaving the picture, hence `route-wraps`. 07's
container measured 13% full, not "well under".

Contrast was measured at the same time (`python scripts/contrast_audit.py`, contrast-audit.md). Every
label and mark in every example is at or above WCAG 2.2's floors in light and dark.

## Second round: Bdo on 03

After the first fixes, 03 still scored 10 and Bdo said the layout "could be far better". It could:

- The labels were a third of their size. The editor caps label size on screen (issue #15), so a
  small diagram fitted for a picture drew them at 4 to 6 world pixels.
- The container's name sat at its foot, squeezed between the child and a dashed guide, and far
  from its own switch glyph.
- The dashed interior guide, an editing aid, was drawn in the picture as a third outline.
- The switch's stem crossed the inner line.

Fixes:

- Pictures (export, PNG, `layout.metrics`, `layout.contrast`) now draw labels at base size whatever
  the editor's zoom (`withPictureLabels`, src/75-persistence.js). The on-screen cap stays.
- A container with a glyph or children is headed by that glyph, drawn small inside its skin, with
  its name under it. The children get the rest.
- The guide shows only while the container is selected or a card is being dragged.
- `empty-container` counts the wires drawn inside as content. 04's route across its frame is not
  empty space.

Next measure: text drawn smaller than a reader can use, measured in the picture's own pixels. It
is carried, not built. The fix above removed the case the examples had.
